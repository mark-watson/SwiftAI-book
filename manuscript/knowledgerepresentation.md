# Knowledge Representation

The material in this chapter is background information on Knowledge Representation and partly a history of some of my work in AI since 1982. There are no programming examples in this chapter.

Knowledge Representation is a sub-field of Artificial Intelligence that addresses the problem of encoding real-world knowledge. Knowledge representation schemes include logic, semantic networks, production rules, and frame representation. We will discuss the classic frame representation first developed by Marvin Minsky in 1975 and then discuss logic and semantic networks as they apply to more modern semantic web and Knowledge Graph projects.

## Frame Systems

Frame data structures collect data for entities, processes, etc. as slot and value pairs. They can be represented by unordered hashtables (maps) and struct data structures as used in Swift, C++, Clojure (defstruct), etc.
